1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BalanceCleaner iOS application and its website. BalanceCleaner helps users reset or clear remaining Apple ID account balances (e.g., prior to changing App Store regions) by utilizing tailored In-App Purchases.

2. Apple ID & Transaction Processing

BalanceCleaner operates strictly within Apple’s StoreKit framework. When clearing your Apple ID balance:

  • Transactions are processed directly and securely by Apple Inc.
  • We do not have access to your Apple ID password, payment cards, or full billing history.
  • Balance deduction is executed as a standard iOS In-App Purchase handled entirely by iOS.

3. Information We Collect (App)

The BalanceCleaner app does not collect your personal information. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive your Apple ID, payment details, or your cleanup history.

4. On-Device Data & iCloud Sync

Your cleanup history and app settings are stored on your device.

iCloud sync (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud, BalanceCleaner can sync your cleanup history between your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). This data is stored in your private iCloud database under your Apple ID. The developer has no access to it, and it is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms. You can turn syncing off or delete this data at any time in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

5. Website & Support Form

This website uses your browser's localStorage to remember your language and theme choices; this stays on your device.

If you contact us through the support form on this website, we receive the name, email address, and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you. The form is processed by FormSubmit, which forwards the message to our email.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Apple Inc.: Processes In-App Purchases and provides iCloud.
  • FormSubmit: Forwards messages sent through the website support form to our email (website only, not used inside the app).

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to access, inspect, or request permanent deletion of any support communications sent to us. Contact us at balancecleanerapp@gmail.com to submit a request.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at balancecleanerapp@gmail.com.
