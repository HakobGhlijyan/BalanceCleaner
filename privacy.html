1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BalanceCleaner iOS application and related web services. BalanceCleaner helps users reset or clear remaining Apple ID account balances (e.g., prior to changing App Store regions) by utilizing tailored In-App Purchases.

2. Apple ID & Transaction Processing

BalanceCleaner operates strictly within Apple’s StoreKit framework. When clearing your Apple ID balance:

  • Transactions are processed directly and securely by Apple Inc.
  • We do not have access to your Apple ID password, payment cards, or full billing history.
  • Balance deduction is executed as a standard iOS In-App Purchase handled entirely by iOS.

3. Information We Collect

We follow a strict data minimization policy:

  • Support Contacts: If you voluntarily reach out via email, we store your email address, name, and message content solely to assist you.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Anonymous crash logs provided automatically by iOS (if enabled in your device settings) to fix software errors.

4. On-Device Data & Local Storage

Application preferences (such as language or theme choices) are stored locally on your device via UserDefaults or browser localStorage. These settings are never transmitted to external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Apple Inc.: Processes In-App Purchases and store infrastructure.
  • FormSubmit: Handles support form inquiries via email encryption.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to access, inspect, or request permanent deletion of any support communications sent to us. Contact us at support@balancecleaner.app to submit a request.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us at support@balancecleaner.app.
