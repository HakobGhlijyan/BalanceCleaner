Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026
In short: BalanceCleaner does not sell personal information. This website collects information only when you voluntarily send feedback or contact the developer.
English

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how BalanceCleaner handles information when you visit this website, view product demos, or contact the developer through the support form.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, and message. Please do not send passwords, payment information, health information, or other sensitive data.

3. How information is used

Information submitted through support is used only to respond to your request, investigate a reported problem, and improve BalanceCleaner. We do not sell or rent personal information.

4. Forms and email

Support messages may be processed by our form and email providers so that the developer can receive and answer them. Those providers may process the information according to their own privacy terms.

5. Analytics and storage

The website does not require an account. The language and theme preferences may be stored locally in your browser using local storage. These preferences are not sent to BalanceCleaner.

6. Data retention and requests

Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, or resolve an issue. To ask about your submitted information or request its deletion, contact support@balancecleaner.app.

Русский

1. Область действия

Эта политика объясняет, как BalanceCleaner обрабатывает информацию при посещении сайта, просмотре демонстрационных материалов или обращении в поддержку.

2. Какие данные вы предоставляете

При обращении вы можете указать имя, email и сообщение. Не отправляйте пароли, платёжные или другие чувствительные данные.

3. Использование данных

Данные используются только для ответа на обращение, проверки проблемы и улучшения BalanceCleaner. Мы не продаём и не сдаём персональную информацию в аренду.

4. Контакт

По вопросам о ваших данных или для запроса удаления напишите на support@balancecleaner.app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the website or data practices change. The date at the top shows when this version was last updated.
