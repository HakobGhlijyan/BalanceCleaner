How Balance Clearing Works

Each tier is a donation that simultaneously zeros your Apple ID balance. The Smart Balance Matcher selects the best combination automatically.

Tier 1 ~$0.99 / ~1 coin

Clears a balance of ~$0.99. Equal to 1 credit coin.

Tier 2 ~$1.99 / ~2 coins

Clears a balance of ~$1.99. Two credit coins.

Tier 3 ~$2.99 / ~3 coins

Use when your balance is around $2–3.

Tier 4 ~$3.99 / ~4 coins

Best for clearing a $3–4 leftover balance.

Tier 5 ~$4.99 / ~5 coins

Handles Apple ID balances around $4–5.

Tier 10 ~$9.99 / ~10 coins

Clears larger balances around $9–10.

💡 Smart Balance Matcher automatically selects the optimal combination of tiers to reach exactly $0.00.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BalanceCleaner?
BalanceCleaner lets you easily select or enter the exact custom amount you want to zero out in your Apple ID account balance — no account registration required.
How does the balance split work?
See the precise breakdown of your remaining balance and calculated parts before confirming any operation. Everything is shown clearly before you proceed.
Is my data safe?
Yes. All processing happens locally on your device via Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not collect, store or upload any personal data.
Do I need an account?
No account is required. Download and start immediately.
What is Pro?
Pro unlocks full transaction history, PDF export, and advanced settings. The core cleaner is free for everyone.
What is the Region Checklist?
A step-by-step guide to prepare your account for a region change. It checks if your Apple ID balance is zero, subscriptions are canceled, and Family Sharing is left.
What Pro customization is available?
Pro users can change the App background to Starfield and customize the Amount Ring Apple Glow Intent Design.
